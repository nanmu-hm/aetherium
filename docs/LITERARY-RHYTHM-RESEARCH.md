# Sentence Rhythm and Literary Research Profiles

Aetherium now models sentence rhythm separately from narrative rhythm.

Narrative rhythm decides the sequence-level breathing state. Sentence rhythm converts scene pressure and expression distance into prose controls such as pace, sentence-length mix, punctuation density, pauses, and paragraph breathing.

Literary research profiles contain abstract mechanisms, constraints, intended effects, and compatible dimensions. They are not author imitation presets.

A built-in Jianghu profile is deliberately mechanism-based: relationship debt, loyalty/freedom tension, hierarchy pressure, and consequence chains. It contains an explicit no-imitation constraint.
