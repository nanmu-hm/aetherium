# Literary Mechanics

Aetherium treats literary quality as a layered system, not a single "style" switch.

## 1. The non-negotiable boundary

The autonomous world is authoritative. Literary expression may not invent, delete, or contradict world facts merely to make prose more dramatic.

The pipeline is:

World Simulation -> Story Discovery -> Narrative Structure -> Literary Expression -> Prose

The first four stages answer different questions:
- World: What actually happened?
- Discovery: Why might this history matter?
- Structure: How should the reader encounter it?
- Expression: How should it be written?

## 2. Literary dimensions

Aetherium should model expression as composable dimensions rather than a small list of author presets:
- language register and cultural texture;
- sentence length and syntactic variation;
- narrative distance and point of view;
- dialogue density;
- character voice;
- emotional explicitness versus restraint;
- subtext;
- imagery and motifs;
- sensory detail;
- exposition density;
- omission and silence;
- humor;
- scene-level rhythm;
- paragraph and sentence cadence.

A work may use different settings in different scenes while preserving a coherent global literary identity.

## 3. Character voice

Every important character should develop a recognizable voice derived from:
- personality and values;
- education and social background;
- emotional state;
- relationship with the listener;
- knowledge and beliefs;
- recurring vocabulary and sentence habits;
- willingness to state feelings directly.

Character voice is not a cosmetic style filter. It is part of characterization.

## 4. Subtext and information layers

The prose generator should distinguish:
- world truth;
- what the viewpoint character knows;
- what the character believes;
- what another character knows;
- what the reader knows.

Dialogue can therefore contain subtext without explicitly narrating the hidden truth.

## 5. Rhythm and breathing

Narrative rhythm and sentence rhythm are coupled but not identical.

Aetherium should support deliberate variation:
calm -> anomaly -> friction -> escalation -> choice -> consequence -> release -> renewal.

High-pressure scenes may use tighter syntax and fewer explanatory digressions; reflective scenes may slow down. The system must avoid maximizing intensity continuously.

## 6. Literary emphasis

Not every event deserves equal prose space.

A narrative resource allocator should decide where to:
- summarize;
- dramatize;
- slow down;
- use dialogue;
- use interiority;
- use environmental detail.

The allocation should depend on causal, relational, emotional, thematic, and character significance, not merely event size.

## 7. Foreshadowing and payoff

Foreshadowing must originate from facts that genuinely exist in the world. The system may notice latent significance later, but should not retroactively plant impossible evidence.

Not every detail needs a payoff. A payoff should preserve causality and gain meaning from what came before.

## 8. Motifs and thematic resonance

Aetherium should allow recurring images, objects, places, phrases, or situations to accumulate meaning through repeated appearances and changed context.

Motifs are observations and expressive structures; they must not override character agency or world causality.

## 9. Literary research profiles

Aetherium may store research profiles describing mechanisms observed in major literary traditions or authors. A profile should decompose observations into abstract mechanisms and constraints.

For example, research on Jin Yong can inform characterization, dialogue, relationship tension, foreshadowing, causal linkage, interleaving story lines, pacing, scene emphasis, and payoff.

The system must not treat an author as a prose imitation preset or reproduce copyrighted passages, characters, or distinctive expression.

## 10. Dynamic expression

Expression should be selected from:
global literary identity
+ genre/world culture
+ narrator/viewpoint
+ character voice
+ scene purpose
+ emotional state
+ narrative pressure
+ information asymmetry
+ pacing state.

This allows one novel to have a coherent identity while its prose breathes differently across scenes.

## 11. Quality gates

Before prose becomes canon, validate:
- factual fidelity to authoritative events;
- viewpoint and knowledge consistency;
- character voice consistency;
- temporal and spatial continuity;
- emotional causality;
- repetition and cliché control;
- rhythm variation;
- unnecessary exposition;
- unresolved setup/payoff integrity.

The literary layer is successful when readers feel the world is alive and the prose makes its history worth experiencing, not when it merely sounds ornate.
